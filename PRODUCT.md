# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sports fans who want to keep up with a match while working, gaming, or creating. They need to see a score or choose a fixture quickly, without turning a lightweight companion into another destination app.

## Product Purpose

SportsPulse is a lightweight Windows desktop companion for tracking live cricket and football fixtures. Its dashboard helps a user choose what to follow; the compact overlay keeps the score visible without interrupting the primary task.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and quietly premium. The interface should feel at home beside Windows 11: familiar, deliberate, and unhurried.

## Anti-references

Avoid a dense admin dashboard, a gamer-overlay aesthetic, loud sport-broadcast graphics, decorative gradients, and custom controls that behave unlike Windows controls.

## Design Principles

1. Stay out of the user’s way until attention is needed.
2. Prefer familiar Windows behavior over clever custom interaction.
3. Use space and hierarchy to make live state instantly legible.
4. Keep the dashboard focused on the next decision: choose or change the tracked fixture.
5. Make every state reachable by mouse and keyboard.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target standard Windows accessibility: keyboard-operable actions, visible focus feedback, non-color-only state cues, and restrained motion that can be reduced.
